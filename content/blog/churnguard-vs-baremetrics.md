## The 30-second answer

**Baremetrics is subscription analytics.** It tells you, beautifully, what your MRR, churn, and LTV are doing. **ChurnGuard is churn prevention.** It tells you *which customers are about to leave or fail a payment* — and helps you save them before the revenue is gone.

They're not the same category. Baremetrics measures the fire; ChurnGuard is the smoke detector. If you mainly need board-ready metrics, Baremetrics is a strong choice. If your actual problem is "customers keep leaving and I find out too late," that's what ChurnGuard was built for.

## What Baremetrics does well (credit where due)

Baremetrics has been around since 2013, and it shows. The dashboards are genuinely best-in-class — MRR movements, cohort retention, LTV breakdowns, forecasting. If you're preparing investor updates or just want gorgeous, trustworthy subscription metrics, it delivers. Their Recover add-on is solid too: automated dunning emails, a hosted card-update widget, and in-app paywalls — and it carries a **guaranteed-ROI promise**: if Recover doesn't recover more revenue than your Baremetrics subscription costs, your next month is free. That's a genuinely confident offer, and it deserves to be acknowledged.

If you're already deep in the Baremetrics ecosystem and happy with the analytics, adding Recover is convenient. Nobody should switch tools just for the sake of switching.

## Where ChurnGuard is different

**1. Prevention, not post-mortems.** A churn rate dashboard tells you that you lost 4.2% last month ([here's what's actually normal for SaaS in 2026](/blog/saas-churn-rate-benchmarks-2026)). ChurnGuard tells you *which specific customers* are at risk right now — with the drivers (failed payments, usage drops, billing issues) and a recommended action for each one. The goal is that next month's churn number never happens.

**2. Everything included, flat price.** With Baremetrics, the retention features are add-ons: Payment Recovery (+$129/mo) and Cancellation Insights (+$129/mo) stack on top of your analytics plan. A founder at $240K ARR pays $333/mo month-to-month for the full stack (or ~$307/mo on annual billing). ChurnGuard includes recovery, insights, and risk scoring in every plan — Growth is $149/mo, all-in, whatever your billing period.

**3. See your own numbers before you pay anything.** The free audit is the honest proof: connect Stripe (read-only) or upload a CSV, and in about 10 minutes you see your churn rate, revenue at risk, and highest-risk customers. If the insights aren't useful, don't subscribe. No credit card, no sales call.

## Who should pick which

**Pick Baremetrics if:** you need deep, board-grade subscription analytics (cohorts, LTV segmentation, forecasting), you bill through multiple processors beyond Stripe, and retention features are secondary to financial reporting.

**Pick ChurnGuard if:** you're a Stripe-based SaaS and your real pain is losing customers — you want per-customer risk scores, failed-payment recovery, and churn insights in one flat-priced tool, and you'd rather prevent churn than admire charts about it.

**Run both if:** you want Baremetrics' analytics depth *and* dedicated churn prevention. Since ChurnGuard starts at $79/mo, many teams keep their analytics stack and add ChurnGuard as the retention layer.
